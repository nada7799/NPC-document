How to get listed

Create your listing

Name, description, logo, website and support email. Pick the markets you serve: UAE, KSA, Egypt.

Declare your capabilities

A capability is something your software does for a seller. Declare only what you actually support — you can add more later.

Prove each one

Each capability lists the steps to complete, with a link to the documentation for every step. Work through them against your sandbox, then upload a zip of your request and response logs.

Submit for review

The FBPI team reads your logs and checks your listing. Every capability has to pass before the listing goes live, so a weak proof holds up the whole listing — not just that capability. If something is rejected you get the reason in the portal, and you can fix it and submit again.

Before you start

Three things you need, none of them set up in the portal.

  • A registered noon Partner Integrator account
  • Your own integrator service account credentials
  • OAuth client credentials — Can be set up through https://access.noon.partners/en/oauth/

The last one is what makes the Connect button work. Without it your listing cannot be published.

How a seller connects

A seller clicks Connect on your listing and lands on a page you host, which starts the OAuth flow. noon shows them a consent screen, they approve, and noon sends an authorization code to your callback.

Your backend exchanges that code for a credential scoped to that one seller.

You use it for all their orders, stock and shipments from then on.

Set up once. It works for every seller after that — nothing is configured per customer.

Worth knowing

Edit your listing whenever you want. Sellers keep seeing your published version until the new one is reviewed and approved, so nothing changes in front of them mid-review.

A capability only appears to sellers once it is verified. Declaring it is not enough.

Proof files are logs from your own sandbox integration, not production seller data.

The portal never asks for your client secret. If anything appears to, it is not us.
